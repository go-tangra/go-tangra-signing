This walk-through takes one PDF from upload to a signed document. You need
*Signing administrator* or *Signing operator* (they can manage templates and
send submissions).

## 1. Upload the PDF

1. Open **Signing → Templates** and click **Upload template**.
2. Pick the **PDF file** (up to 50 MB, not encrypted), give it a **Name**
   and click **Upload**.

The template starts as a **Draft** and the builder opens. Later, click the
template's row to open it again.

## 2. Place the fields

The builder shows the pages in the middle, the **Fields** palette and the
**Parties** on the left, and the selected field's properties on the right.

1. Under **Parties**, rename **First party** to, for example, `Signer`.
2. Drag **Signature** from the palette onto the spot where the signature
   goes (or click the type, then the spot). Resize it with its handles.
3. Optionally click **Auto-detect fields**: dotted or underlined placeholders
   become proposed text fields. Remove the ones you do not need.
4. Click **Save**, then **Activate**.

## 3. Send it

1. Open **Signing → Submissions** and click **New submission**.
2. Choose the **Template**, and pick yourself for the `Signer` party.
3. Click **Create**, then **Send now**.

You receive an e-mail invitation, and the document appears in **To sign**.

## 4. Sign it

1. Open **Signing → To sign** and click **Open** on the document.
2. The first time, the page asks you to **Set up your certificate**: choose a
   PIN (at least 6 characters) on **My certificate**, click **Create
   certificate**, then **Back to the document**.
3. Draw your signature under **Your signature**, or type your name.
4. Click **Sign with my certificate** and enter your PIN.

The submission is now **Completed**. Its page offers the **Final signed
document** and, a moment later, the **Audit trail**. Check the signature on
**Signing → Verify**.
